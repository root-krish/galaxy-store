# 🏆 Sports Galaxy
> **Elevating the Online Sports Retail Experience.**

Sports Galaxy is a robust, web-based e-commerce and inventory management system designed to modernize retail administration. Say goodbye to manual record-keeping and hello to real-time stock tracking, secure dispatching, and intelligent financial analytics. 

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🛒 For Customers
* **Seamless Shopping:** Browse products with real-time live search and filtering.
* **Smart Cart:** Automatic tax calculations and dynamic bill updates.
* **Instant Receipts:** Printable digital invoices generated the moment an order is placed.

### 💼 For Store Admins
* **Automated Inventory:** Real-time stock deductions and low-stock dashboard alerts (triggers when items drop below 5 units).
* **Financial Analytics:** Track daily sales, total transactions, and gross revenue at a glance.
* **Profit Tracking:** Automated monthly net profit calculations based on operational expenses and purchase costs.

### 🚚 For Delivery Partners
* **Mobile-Optimized Dashboard:** Check order addresses and update delivery milestones on the go.
* **Secure Handover:** Shared verification codes between the customer and delivery partner ensure orders reach the right hands.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

**Frontend Interface & Logic**
* **HTML5:** Semantic structuring for product catalogs and billing forms.
* **CSS3:** Custom dashboard layouts, highlight styling for low-stock tables, and responsive mobile views.
* **JavaScript:** Instant checkout math, live database search, and client-side form validation.

**Backend & Architecture**
* **PHP:** Secure role-based access control, transaction processing, and business logic execution.
* **MySQL:** Centralized relational database handling inventory state, sales history, and user credentials.
